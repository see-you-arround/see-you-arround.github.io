About DmFirst

DmFirst Official· Updated September 2026

This page is the official information site for DmFirst. The games themselves run at dmfirst11.com — a web and Android platform offering WinGo, colour prediction, K3, 5D, Aviator, TRX WinGo, and a slots section, built for players in India.

This site publishes the details that come up most: how to log in, how to register with an invitation code, what each game pays, how withdrawals work, and how to spot fake copies of the platform.

Why this site exists

Registration on DmFirst is invite-only. Every new account needs an active invitation code, and the only source we publish codes from is this site. That's the reason this page exists — one place where the code, the rules, and the answers to common problems all live, instead of scattered across Telegram groups and third-party blogs that recycle stale information.

What we cover

  • How to register with an invitation code
  • How to log in, and how to reset a password
  • The WinGo 30s payout table, with the exact per-round multipliers
  • Colour prediction rules
  • What counts as an illegal bet, and why the rule exists
  • Deposit and withdrawal limits — ₹100 to ₹50,000 for deposits, ₹500 to ₹50,000 per withdrawal, three withdrawals per day
  • How to change your bank account or USDT withdrawal address
  • Common support issues and how to fix them
  • How to protect yourself from fake sites in this category

What we don't do

We don't handle support for account-specific issues on this page. If your deposit didn't credit, your withdrawal was rejected, or your password reset isn't working, the fastest route is the blue Customer Service icon inside the DmFirst app or website. The support team can see your account details and transaction history — this page can't.

If the in-app CS icon isn't reachable, you can also emailsupport@dmfirst11.com. Response times are slower than in-app, so use it as a fallback rather than the primary route.

We also don't make promises about winnings. Colour prediction and the other games on DmFirst are structured so the platform comes out ahead over thousands of rounds. They're entertainment. Anyone telling you otherwise — including anyone claiming to represent DmFirst — is wrong.

Domain verification

Before you enter login details, deposit money, or add bank information on any site claiming to be DmFirst, verify the URL. The domain check atdmfirst.comshows the current list of official DmFirst addresses. If the site you're on isn't on that list, close it.

If something on this site is out of date — a rule that's changed, a withdrawal limit that no longer matches the platform, or a game detail that's wrong — emailadmin@dmfirst.comand we'll correct it. Small inaccuracies cost users real money, so we treat reports seriously.
