What is DmFirst colour prediction?

Colour prediction is the game most players come to DmFirst for. Here's how a round works:

  • You pick a colour — Green, Red, or Violet.
  • You stake an amount on that pick.
  • A timer counts down, usually 30 to 60 seconds.
  • A result is drawn. If your colour comes up, you get paid at the displayed odds. If it doesn't, the stake is gone.

Green and Red typically pay 2x. Violet pays more because it comes up less often. The payouts are set so the platform profits over thousands of rounds. Fine for entertainment. Not designed for steady returns.

How DmFirst handles your money and data

Payments run through UPI. That means your card number and bank login never touch the platform — the transaction goes through your UPI app the same way a food delivery order would.

Withdrawals return to the same UPI ID you deposited from. That's a standard anti-fraud measure: it stops someone who's compromised an account from redirecting money elsewhere.
