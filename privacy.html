About Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026 · Last updated 23 September 2026

This policy covers how the DmFirst information site at dmfirst55.com handles data. The DmFirst platform itself — the games, deposits, withdrawals, and account system — runs at dmfirst11.com and operates under its own privacy terms. This policy is only about what happens when you visit this page.

Information we collect

This site is a static page. It has no user accounts, login forms, or data submission of its own. There are three things it collects, all indirectly:

  • Server logs. The hosting provider records basic technical data when someone visits — IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and time of visit. Standard for any website, and used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Analytics. If analytics are enabled, we collect aggregated information about how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and where they came from. This is anonymous and doesn't identify you.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us at the address below, we receive whatever you include in that message.

How we use this information

  • To keep the site running and secure
  • To understand which pages are useful and which need work
  • To respond to messages you send
  • To detect and prevent abuse

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't build profiles about you.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies. If analytics are enabled, they may set a cookie to distinguish new visitors from returning ones. These cookies don't contain personal information and can be blocked in your browser without affecting your ability to use the site.

If you've arrived here from the DmFirst platform or another linked site, that site may have set its own cookies. Those are governed by that site's privacy terms, not this one.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may use analytics services. Both are standard third-party services with their own privacy terms:

  • Google Fonts serves the typography. Google may log the request, including the IP address from which it originated.
  • Analytics services, if used, record aggregated visitor behaviour. They don't receive personal information from us.

We don't share information with these services beyond what happens automatically when a page loads.

Your choices

  • Block cookies in your browser settings at any time
  • Use browser extensions that block analytics and tracking scripts
  • Email us to ask what information we hold about you

Because this site doesn't have user accounts, there's usually nothing personal tied to you to request or delete. If you've emailed us, you can ask us to remove that correspondence.

Security

The site loads over HTTPS. Data between your browser and the server is encrypted in transit. The site doesn't store personal information on its servers — there's no database with user records, because there's no user system here.

The DmFirst platform handles payments and account data under its own security practices. See the platform for those details.

Children

This site is intended for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will be noted. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about this policy, or about data on this site, can go to admin@dmfirst.com with "Privacy" in the subject line. For anything about the game platform itself — your account, deposits, or withdrawals — use the in-app support or email support@dmfirst11.com.
